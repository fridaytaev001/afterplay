Choose your promotion

An opponent with a memory.

1. Import recent public Chess.com games or upload a PGN. For a PGN, enter the exact White/Black player name.

2. Inspect measured habits, then build an AI counter-plan.

3. Play against the model, or compare a decision with and without that memory.

The model sees an image of the current board alongside the position, game history, and legal candidates. It selects a candidate and explains its choice. This is a prototype, not a rated chess engine.

To enable AI, set AI_GATEWAY_API_KEY in the server’s environment. AI_MODEL optionally selects a vision-capable Vercel AI Gateway model. Redeploy after configuring Vercel.
